## Posting

One post when you publish the portfolio. Lead with the finding, not the launch.

> I spent [N] weeks building a data analysis portfolio. The most useful thing I
> learned came from a calculation that killed my own plan.
>
> I'd designed a winback campaign: target the 930 lapsed high-value customers,
> send to 70%, hold back 30% as control. Sensible.
>
> Then I ran the power analysis. To detect the lift we cared about I needed
> 1,209 customers per arm. A 30% holdback of 930 is 279.
>
> That design could only have detected a 7-percentage-point lift — more than
> doubling the reactivation rate. If the campaign had worked exactly as hoped,
> it would have come back "not statistically significant", and we'd have
> concluded winback doesn't work.
>
> The fix was unglamorous: widen the target, and split 50/50 instead of 70/30,
> because power is driven by the smaller arm. Same effect, p = 0.003 instead of
> p = 0.12.
>
> Running the numbers before the campaign cost an afternoon. Running the wrong
> campaign would have cost the idea.
>
> Full write-up: [link]

Then stop. One good post beats six announcements.
